Group lessons

$55 per session for the public, $50 for FGCU affiliates. Registration closes the Friday before a session starts, at 3pm; late registration by phone ((239) 590-7701) adds a $10 fee.

Water Babies

6 months to 3 years, with a parent or guardian in the water.

Preschool

Ages 3 to 5, in beginner, intermediate and advanced levels.

Learn-to-Swim

Ages 6 to 13, in four levels.

Adult

13 and older, from beginner to stroke performance.

Not sure of the level? Ask for an in-person swim test with a Water Safety Instructor: (239) 590-7701.

Private lessons

Half-hour lessons, one-on-one or for two swimmers of similar ability, scheduled with your instructor during open hours (not during group lessons).
