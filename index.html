Dive in — We're Here for the Whole Community

The Lee County/FGCU Aquatics Center opened in 2004 with over 1 million gallons of water. The 50-meter pool hosts meets and practices and serves the community's lap swimmers; the 25-yard pool and diving well is for recreational swimmers and divers.

The water

50-meter pool

8 long-course lanes, or 20 short-course lanes with the bulkhead in. Which layout it's in shows on the lane calendar.

25-yard pool & diving well

8 lanes, with 1-meter and 3-meter diving boards.

Heated and chilled

Pool water is heated or chilled depending on the season. Locker rooms, a family restroom with shower, loungers and picnic tables.

Getting in

FGCU students get in with their Eagle ID. Faculty, staff, Southwest Florida residents and visitors pay a daily fee or buy a membership.

Policies

General policies
  • Check in at the front desk with a valid FGCU ID or pass.
  • Children younger than 13 must be under direct adult supervision; children younger than 5 must have an adult with them in the water.
  • No food, glass, gum, tobacco or alcohol on deck; plastic water bottles with a lid are fine.
  • Return borrowed kickboards, paddles, pull buoys and aqua joggers to the front desk.
  • No rain passes or refunds when weather closes the facility.
Pool policies
  • A lifeguard must be on duty. Lap swimmers may have to share lanes and circle swim at busy times.
  • Swimwear is required; infants and toddlers need swim diapers.
  • No running, rough play, starting blocks, diving into shallow water, swimming under the bulkhead, or hypoxic breath-holding.
Diving board policies
  • Children 13 and younger may need to pass a swim test first. The 3-meter board needs staff permission.
  • One person on the board; one bounce; forward dives only; leave the diving area right away.
  • The diving well may close for programs or open swim.

What this replaces

  • Hours in two places that don't know about each other. The Operating Hours page lists the fall open swim hours and a hand-typed "Upcoming Adjusted Hours" list (on October 23 it still listed October 21 and 22). The lane calendar is a separate Google Calendar. Here the hours, the adjusted days and the lane counts are one schedule: the hours box above is computed from it.
  • A lane calendar a week deep. Lane counts are posted about a week ahead; beyond that the calendar holds only "POOL CLOSED" on Sundays, into January.
  • Lane counts as typed titles. Each block is a title like "25Y: 4 Lanes", typed as "25Y", "25 Y" and "25  Y", "SC" and "SCY", "50M" and "50m". Some October blocks said "50M: 10 LC Lanes", but FGCU's visitor page gives the 50-meter pool 8 long-course lanes. Here a block is booked on real lanes in the pool's real layout, so the count can't disagree with the pool.
  • Which pool is which, by color. The calendar tells the pools apart only by green and blue, and the page says to phone (239) 590-7700 to learn whether the 50-meter pool is short course or long course. Here each block names its pool and layout.
  • Courses on a third page. The lifeguard course dates are only on the Certification Courses page, not on the lane calendar or the hours, so nothing shows that a course is in the water on a Saturday when open swim is also listed.
