Lifeguard (American Red Cross, blended)

Prepares you to prevent, recognize and respond to aquatic and medical emergencies, and to give care until EMS arrives. Attendance is required on every day of a course.

Coming course days: …

Cost: $200 FGCU students, faculty and staff; $225 community.

Prerequisites
  • Swim 150 yards continuously (front crawl, breaststroke or both, face in the water), tread water 2 minutes with hands in armpits, then swim 50 more yards.
  • Swim 20 yards, surface dive 7–10 feet, bring up a 10-pound brick and swim 20 yards back on your back holding it, without goggles.
  • Finish the online course before the first day and bring the completion printout.

Water Safety Instructor (WSI)

Required to teach swim lessons; covers the American Red Cross Swimming and Water Safety program.

Coming course days: …

Cost: $200 FGCU students; $225 community.

Prerequisites
  • 25 yards each of front crawl, back crawl, breaststroke, elementary backstroke and sidestroke; 15 yards of butterfly.
  • 1 minute each of back float and treading water.
  • Finish the online course before the first day and bring the completion printout.
